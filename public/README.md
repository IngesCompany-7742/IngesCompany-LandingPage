# DoofPlus – Pharmaceutical Quality Management Platform

## Project Overview

**DoofPlus** is a web platform developed by **IngesCompany** to support quality management and traceability within the pharmaceutical industry.
The solution centralizes information related to manufacturing and quality control processes, facilitating access to organized, reliable records available for consultation and follow-up.

DoofPlus aims to strengthen the management of processes associated with the lifecycle of pharmaceutical products, supporting documentation, traceability, and regulatory compliance activities. This platform contributes to better visibility of the information required for decision-making and favors compliance with Good Manufacturing Practices (BPM) and standards demanded by the sector's regulatory entities.

---

## 1. Antecedentes (Background)

The pharmaceutical industry is among the most regulated sectors due to the direct impact its products have on public health. In Peru, the General Directorate of Medicines, Supplies and Drugs (DIGEMID) demands compliance with Good Manufacturing Practices (BPM), which establish guidelines related to production, quality control, documentation, and process management.

Furthermore, international organizations such as the World Health Organization (WHO) emphasize that data traceability and integrity are fundamental for pharmaceutical quality management. The availability of complete, accurate, and accessible information supports audits, validation, and regulatory compliance.

In this context, digital transformation represents an opportunity to optimize information management during pharmaceutical manufacturing. Integrating digital platforms with automatic data capture technologies (IoT) strengthens batch traceability and facilitates access to critical information for decision-making.

---

## 2. Problemática (Problem Statement)

- **Gestión compleja de la documentación de calidad:** Quality assurance generates vast amounts of information (protocols, production records, analysis results, deviations). Managing this across distributed sources makes tracking evidence challenging.
- **Dificultades en la trazabilidad de lotes farmacéuticos:** Traceability is essential to reconstruct a product's manufacturing history. Integrating data from different stages is complex, limiting visibility over the lifecycle of each batch.
- **Necesidad de fortalecer la integridad y disponibilidad de la información:** The WHO highlights data integrity as an essential component. Inadequate management of records can compromise process reliability and complicate audits.
- **Oportunidad de utilización de tecnologías IoT:** IoT allows direct data capture from equipment, complementing quality records with automatically obtained data, thus increasing reliability.

---

## 3. Análisis 5W & 2H

- **What (¿Qué?):** Resolve limitations related to quality documentation management, pharmaceutical batch traceability, and the availability of reliable information for QA and regulatory compliance.
- **Why (¿Por qué?):** Because medication quality depends on adequately controlled, documented processes backed by complete and traceable information, facilitating audits and BPM compliance.
- **Who (¿Quién?):** QA/QC specialists and pharmaceutical production supervisors managing documentation and ensuring regulatory standards.
- **When (¿Cuándo?):** During all stages of a pharmaceutical batch lifecycle, including manufacturing, quality control, deviation management, and audits.
- **Where (¿Dónde?):** In laboratories and pharmaceutical plants executing production, QA, and regulatory control activities.
- **How (¿Cómo?):** Through a digital platform that centralizes protocols, quality dossiers, and batch records, complementing information with IoT technologies.
- **How much (¿Cuánto?):** Implementation requires technological infrastructure, document digitization, user training, and IoT integration. Investment depends on the organization's size.

## Target Audience

### Segment 1: Quality Assurance and Control Specialist (QA/QC)
- **Age:** Between 30 and 55 years.
- **Occupation:** Professional responsible for guaranteeing compliance with quality standards, managing technical documentation, supervising deviations, and participating in regulatory audits.
- **Background:** Pharmaceutical Chemist, Pharmaceutical Engineering or related fields with specialization in QA, BPM, or pharmaceutical regulation.
- **Key Need:** Maintain complete, traceable, and available records for inspection and audit activities. Transform digital workflows to centralize quality information from multiple sources.

### Segment 2: Pharmaceutical Production Manager or Supervisor
- **Age:** Between 35 and 70 years.
- **Occupation:** Professional responsible for planning, supervising, and controlling pharmaceutical manufacturing operations.
- **Background:** Industrial Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Pharmaceutical Engineering.
- **Key Need:** Continuous control of operational variables to guarantee the quality of the final product. Adopt Industry 4.0 digital tools to improve visibility of production processes and document activities according to BPM.

---

## Key Features

- **Real-Time IoT Monitoring:** Automated capture of temperature, pressure, and pH from industrial equipment.
- **Automated BPM Compliance Engine:** Evaluates every sensor reading against configured GMP parameters.
- **Immutable Audit Trails:** Timestamped and tamper-proof actions.
- **Digital Batch Management:** Full lifecycle management of production batches.
- **Instant Deviation Alerts:** Push notifications, email, or SMS when a critical variable goes out of range.
- **KPI Analytics Dashboard:** Conformance rates and deviation trends.
